What we need

Useful help is specific, finite, and aligned with the rules.

We are not looking for vague hype. We need reviewable contributions that move the first public proof toward a safe 100-user adult pilot.

Developers Shield API sketches, assertion verification, passkey prototypes, frontend, media upload, feeds, export, accessibility, and tests. Designers Onboarding, feed, upload, explicit-content controls, report/appeal, creator dashboard, and capacity UI. Privacy and security reviewers Minimal assertions, pairwise pseudonyms, provider boundaries, threat models, data inventory, upload abuse, and launch boundaries. Creators Cross-post rights-clean original content later and help test whether creator economics are understandable. Organizations Offer measured resources, research help, legal review, moderation expertise, credits, or pilot partnerships. Universities and professors Contribute research review, student projects, privacy-preserving identity methods, safety methods, sustainability measurement, or public-interest labs.

Future platform

What the platform should offer over time.

EverCommons starts with social because photo/video social networks shape culture and attention. Voxon Shield should become the shared trust layer for future applications that need human, age, eligibility, messaging, or abuse assertions.

Voxon Shield

A reusable open-source trust layer for verified-human, age, passkey, pseudonymity, anti-spam, and policy assertions.

Open social media

Profiles, media, comments, feeds, creator pages, transparent discovery, and export.

Creator and contributor economics

Shadow ledgers first, then published formulas for creators, maintainers, infrastructure, safety, reserves, and public benefit.

Safety and family controls

Explicit-content controls, reporting, blocking, appeals, anti-spam systems, and age-aware design before broad launch.

Open data utilities

Future commons tools should make useful information easier to inspect without depending on opaque platforms.

Sustainable infrastructure

Measure cost, energy, storage, egress, provider concentration, and environmental intensity per useful activity.

Real community partnerships

Universities, nonprofits, cooperatives, and responsible companies can help without buying user data or governance control.
