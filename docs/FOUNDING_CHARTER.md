# Founding Charter

Status: draft for public review.

EverCommons exists to build and maintain open, private, interoperable, and economically sustainable digital services that serve users, creators, contributors, and the public interest.

## Commitments

1. Privacy is for everyone. Essential privacy protections are not conditional on payment.
2. No sale of people. EverCommons will not sell personal data, operate a cross-site tracking network, or use covert fingerprinting to create advertising profiles.
3. Open core. Mission-critical software and specifications will remain available under approved open-source licenses, subject only to narrow and time-limited security exceptions.
4. User freedom. Users will receive documented export, deletion, and migration paths.
5. Transparent money. Revenue definitions, allocation formulas, aggregate payouts, provider contributions, and material changes will be published.
6. Fair participation. Creators and eligible contributors can participate in economic value according to published rules, due process, and anti-fraud controls.
7. Replaceable providers. No infrastructure, AI, payment, advertising, or communications provider may receive ownership of user data or become contractually irreplaceable.
8. Capacity-aligned growth. Registration pauses when technical, safety, support, legal, or financial capacity falls outside published limits.
9. Safety and rights. Reporting, reasoned enforcement, appeals, transparency, and age-appropriate safeguards are product requirements.
10. Public benefit. At least 1% of annual revenue is reserved for independently verified environmental or public-interest causes once revenue exists.
11. Fair work. Founders, employees, and maintainers may be paid well. Precarity is not required for public-interest technology.
12. Mission lock. Material changes to privacy, openness, portability, public benefit, or economic participation require public notice, independent oversight, and supermajority approval once formal governance exists.
13. Honest communication. EverCommons distinguishes projects it uses, projects that inspire it, community contributions, sponsors, and official contractual partners.
14. Evidence. EverCommons publishes regular reports on product quality, safety, privacy, finances, infrastructure concentration, and environmental intensity once those systems exist.

## Current Boundary

EverCommons is pre-alpha. This charter is not a legal entity, securities document, donation solicitation, employment offer, or partnership agreement.
