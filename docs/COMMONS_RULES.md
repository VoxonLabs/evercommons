# Commons Rules

Status: draft standard, not a legal certification yet.

Commons Rules is the future compatibility standard for VoxonLabs and EverCommons projects. It is the answer to: "What does a project have to follow to be trusted as part of this ecosystem?"

## Public Meaning

A project that follows Commons Rules should be able to say:

```text
Built under Commons Rules: open core, privacy by default, child-aware safety, portability, transparent money, no surveillance business model, and public-benefit alignment.
```

Until legal review and governance exist, this is a project standard, not a registered trademark, certification mark, or external endorsement programme.

## Baseline Rules

1. Open core: mission-critical code and specifications are public under approved open-source licences.
2. Privacy by default: privacy is not a premium feature and personal data is not sold.
3. No surveillance ads: advertising, if used, is contextual and clearly labelled.
4. Portability: users can export meaningful data and leave without artificial lock-in.
5. Safety by design: reporting, blocking, moderation reasons, appeals, and abuse controls are part of the product.
6. Child-aware defaults: products that may reach minors must include age-aware design, explicit-content controls, and specialist review before broad launch.
7. Explicit-content protection: general-audience products must make pornographic and highly explicit material controllable by default and clearly separated from ordinary discovery.
8. Transparent economics: creator, contributor, infrastructure, reserve, safety, and public-benefit allocations use published formulas when money exists.
9. Replaceable providers: no vendor receives user-data ownership, algorithmic control, or permanent revenue rights.
10. Public-benefit link: every product should support healthier digital society, sustainability, or another concrete public-interest purpose.
11. Minimal trust assertions: applications request derived facts, not raw identity documents, dates of birth, addresses, or provider packets.
12. Scoped enforcement: access limits, suspensions, and abuse decisions are app-scoped where possible, reasoned, time-bounded, reviewable, and never reduced to a shared global social-credit score.

## First Product Under the Rules

EverCommons Social is the first product concept: an open-source photo/video social network for real people, creators, communities, and public-interest projects.

It should begin with adult closed testing, strong reporting and moderation tools, explicit-content controls, a following feed, transparent discovery, creator dashboards, and no public upload scale until capacity gates pass. Where identity or age assurance is needed, EverCommons should rely on the Voxon Shield model: short-lived assertions instead of raw identity data.

## Inspiration Boundary

EverCommons respects projects such as Signal, GitHub, VideoLAN, and many open-source communities because they demonstrate useful public lessons: privacy discipline, open collaboration, durable media infrastructure, and long-term maintenance.

This does not mean they support, endorse, certify, or partner with EverCommons. Official partnership requires explicit written agreement.
