# EverCommons Manifesto

EverCommons exists to prove that a polished digital network can be free to use without turning people into inventory.

We are not anti-business, anti-advertising, or anti-profit. We are against surveillance economics, hidden lock-in, unpaid maintenance, unsafe growth, and public-interest language that collapses when money arrives.

The first product is deliberately small: EverCommons Social, an open-source photo/video social pilot where creators can cross-post, users can participate without being tracked across the web, contributors can do finite useful work, and infrastructure providers can support measured capacity without owning the network.

## The Public Promise

We will grow only as fast as we can remain fast, private, safe, financially responsible, and faithful to the commons.

## What We Refuse

- Selling personal data.
- Cross-site surveillance advertising.
- Making privacy a premium feature.
- Secret creator or contributor formulas.
- Provider lock-in disguised as partnership.
- Growth that outruns moderation, support, legal, financial, or technical capacity.
- Founder or sponsor control that can quietly reverse the mission.

## What We Build Toward

- Open core software and documented protocols.
- Portable user data and replaceable providers.
- Contextual, clearly labelled advertising rather than behavioural surveillance.
- Transparent creator, contributor, infrastructure, reserve, safety, and public-benefit pools.
- A service normal people can use without needing to understand open-source governance.

This starts as a proof, not a claim of victory. A trustworthy 100-user pilot is more valuable than a million-person promise.
