# Code of Conduct

EverCommons is intended to become a public-interest project with users, creators, contributors, sponsors, and affected communities. The collaboration standard starts now.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, respectful, and specific.
- Criticize ideas and decisions without attacking people.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when discussing money, governance, vendors, safety, or moderation.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information without consent.
- Assume public records may be read by future contributors, creators, partners, journalists, or regulators.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or sexualized attention.
- Doxing or pressure to disclose private identity information.
- Abusive behavior toward moderators, maintainers, users, or creators.
- Spam, fraud, astroturfing, undisclosed paid advocacy, or manipulation of contribution records.
- Claims of official partner, maintainer, or governance status that have not been granted.

## Enforcement

During pre-alpha, reports should go to the project maintainer. As the project grows, enforcement should move to a documented moderation process with reasoned decisions, appeals, and transparency reporting.

This document is a starting point, not a substitute for a mature trust and safety policy.
